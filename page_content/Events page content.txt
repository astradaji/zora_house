What's Happening at the House

Calendar Overview
Here’s a snapshot of events taking place at Zora’s House. Each event is hosted by either our organization or one of our Collaborative partner organizations. Most events are open to all to attend, while some are specifically for our Members. To let us know you’re coming, follow the registration prompt on each event’s page on Eventbrite, Zora’s World, or other partner event platforms. Keep scrolling to find out what’s happening at the House!
