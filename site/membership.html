Membership

More than access. Membership at Zora’s House means belonging.

Become A Member Sponsor A Membership
Overview

Zora’s House membership is for women and gender-expansive people of color who want to go deeper into community, into connection, into a space that was built with you in mind. Members get priority access to programming, members-only events and discounts, connection to our curated network via our online community, Zora’s World, and a community of women who just get it.

At a glance

There’s a particular kind of exhaustion that comes from spending your days in spaces not designed for you. Zora’s House was built as the antidote to that.

Our membership is a home for women+ of color who want to be known, supported, and celebrated, not despite who they are, but because of it. As a member, you’re not just joining a space. You’re joining a movement eight years in the making, a community of thousands of women across Central Ohio, and a building full of people who are figuring things out together.

Who it’s for

Women and gender-expansive people of color (women+)

Priority programming, members-only events and discounts, and Zora’s World

Sponsor a membership

How It Works

Membership is pay-what-you-can (suggested $40/month) and open to women and gender-expansive people of color. We also invite people beyond the WOC+ community to sponsor a membership, granting Central Ohio women+ of color access to our curated community.

Who It’s For

Membership is for women of color and gender-expansive people of color (women+). Allies and friends are welcome to sponsor a membership to provide a woman+ of color in our community with the ability to connect more deeply with her community through our organization.

FAQ

Is Zora’s House just for women+ of color?

Zora’s House centers women+ of color, which means everything we do is designed around the needs and lived experiences of women+ of color. But our space is also meant to be a space for the entire community to learn about (and celebrate!) the contributions, history, and experiences of women+ of color.

Do you have to be a member to enter Zora’s House?

You don’t have to be a member to participate in the bulk of our events and programs. Members do, however, receive access to special events, discounts, and our online community, Zora’s World!

How do you define women+ of color?

We recognize that race and gender are labels (that we didn’t get a whole lot of say in creating) and that each of us experiences them in different ways. For that reason, we don’t define “women of color.” We allow the people we serve to claim this identity for themselves.
